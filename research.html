Research overview

My research uses observations of transiting giant exoplanets to study their atmospheres. I’m particularly interested in atmospheric escape and evolution, and in how observations across ultraviolet, optical, and infrared wavelengths can reveal the processes shaping these worlds.

Research projects

I study a range of questions about giant exoplanet atmospheres, including atmospheric escape, clouds, and differences between planets’ morning and evening sides. More details about individual projects will be added here.
